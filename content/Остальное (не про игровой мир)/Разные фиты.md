**Minor Wizard**

*Prerequisites: Intelligence 13 or higher*
You decide to study magic from old folios and textbooks or from the words of ancient wizards. However, you don't have much time, so you only have time to learn the basics.

1. Bonus Spells: You get access to 1 additional Level 1 wizard spell and 1 wizard spell. These spells do not count towards your limit of known or prepared spells and can be cast using your existing spell cells. The basic spell characteristic for these spells and those recorded in the arcane book is Intelligence.

2. Magic Focus: You gain the ability to use a wizard's magic focus(wand, staff, or similar item). This allows you to ignore spell components at no cost, providing you with convenience and flexibility when casting spells.

3. Creating an arched book: You can create an arcane book (similar to a wizard's spell book). Writing spells in an arcane book requires the same time and cost as for wizards, but its volume is 2 times less (instead of 100 pages, there are only 50).


You can write and copy spells from scrolls to the arcane book (regardless of your class) that you encounter or that you study. This requires the time and cost of components similar to recording spells by a wizard.
You can use your arcane book to prepare spells as if you were a wizard. The number of spells you can prepare is equal to your caster level + Intelligence modifier (minimum 1).
If you lose the arcane book, you can recreate it from memory. It takes 1 hour for each spell level recorded in the book and requires components worth 50 gold coins per spell level.
  
**Multi-Attacker**

*Prerequisites: Strength or Dexterity 13 or higher*
You are capable of multiple swift strikes against your opponents, gaining the following benefit:

- Once on each of your turns when you take the Attack action, you can make one additional attack as a part of the same action. You may use this effect a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all uses of this feat after you finish a long rest.   


**Mystic Initiate**

*Prerequisites: Intelligence 13 or higher*
You learn one psionic talent and one psionic discipline from the list available to the mystic class. Your manifestation ability is Intelligence. You have 2 psi points that you can use with your chosen discipline, and you regain spent psi points when you complete a long rest.

**Armored Arcanist**

*Prerequisites: The ability to cast at least one spell*
The ebb and flow of combat has taught you to infuse your armor with your arcane power. You gain the following effects:

- Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1 to a maximum of 20.
- You can use your spellcasting ability modifier, replacing Dexterity or Strength, when calculating your AC. Any limits applied by the armor or unarmored defense feature is still in place, such as medium armor having a maximum bonus of +2.
- While wearing heavy armor, you can reduce the damage you take by your spellcasting ability modifier once per turn.  


**Oracle**

*Prerequisites: Intelligence or Wisdom 15 or higher*
Glimpses of the future begin to press in on your awareness, allowing you to potentially change fate.

When you finish a long rest, roll two d20 and record the numbers rolled. You can replace any attack roll, saving throw, or ability check made by you or a creature that you can see with one of these foretelling rolls. You must choose to do so before the roll, and you can replace a roll in this way only once per turn. Each foretelling roll can be used only once. When you finish a long rest, you lose any unused foretelling rolls. 

`По сути просто взяли способность волшебника школы прорицания второго уровня`  

**Overcharged Lightning**

*Prerequisites: Spellcasting or Pact Magic feature, ability to deal lightning damage*

Your practiced electromancy is far more dangerous than the average lighting bolt.

When you expend a spell slot to deal lightning damage to a creature, it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw. The DC is equal to 8 + your spellcasting modifier + your proficiency bonus. On a fail, the creature is paralyzed until the end of its next turn.

Additionally, when you deal lightning damage to a creature, all creatures within 5 ft of the target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a fail, the target takes half of the lightning damage rolled.

**Quickness**

You can react quickly to any situation. You gain the following benefits:

- Add your proficiency bonus to your initiative.
- Your base movement speed is increased by 15 feet.

**Scope Adept**

*Prerequisites: The ability to cast at least one spell*
You have learned a variety of highly experimental spellcasting techniques which enlarge the scope of your spells. You gain the following benefits:
- When you cast a spell which requires a ranged attack roll, you can enlarge the scope of the spell, altering it to explode and affect a small burst. When you do so, you can choose to attack a point within range instead of a creature or object. If you do directly hit a creature, however, it takes full (i.e. normal) damage from the spell and is not allowed a Dexterity saving throw. Each creature within 5 feet of the target must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking half of the spell's damage on a failed save, or no damage on a successful one.
- You learn one cantrip that requires a ranged attack roll. Choose one cantrip from the bard, cleric, druid, sorcerer, warlock or wizard spell list. Your spellcasting ability for this cantrip depends on the spell list you chose from: Charisma for bard, sorcerer, or warlock; Wisdom for cleric or druid; or Intelligence for wizard.

**Secondary Archetype Initiate**

You can take a feature from a different archetype/subclass in your class, as long as you are at a level that can use the feature. You can take this feat more than once, but you cannot take the same feature.

**Shaper of Earth**

*Prerequisites: Knows the mold earth cantrip*

With experience and training, you have become well-versed in the ways of manipulating elemental earth, expanding the abilities of your mold earth cantrip. It gains the following additional abilities:
- You forcefully move a portion of loose earth or loose stone up to 5 feet in any direction. If this movement would strike a creature, that creature must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 1d6 bludgeoning damage on a failed save.
- You lift up and throw a portion of loose earth or stone, weighing no more than your spellcasting ability modifier in pounds, at a target within range. Make a ranged spell attack with this projectile, dealing bludgeoning damage equal to 1d6 plus your spellcasting modifier on a hit.
- You shape and harden loose earth or loose stone into a bludgeoning weapon with weight equal to the material used. This weapon is nonmagical, and lasts for up to 1 hour.
- Over the process of 1 minute, you clad yourself in 90 pounds of loose earth or stone, donning stone armor. This armor lasts for up to 1 hour.
- As a reaction, you can brace your feet in earth or stone, adding your proficiency bonus to checks and saving throws made against being pushed, pulled, or knocked prone until the beginning of your next turn.
- You lift a portion of loose earth or loose stone and move it up to 5 feet in any direction. You can keep this portion and any other material within or atop it aloft for as long as you concentrate. The maximum weight you can lift with this ability (in pounds) is equal to your spellcasting ability score multiplied by your proficiency bonus, times 20.
- You burrow through earth or stone, leaving a tunnel in your wake. To tunnel through 5 feet of material, it takes 1 action for soft soil, 1 minute for tough earth, and 10 minutes for hard stone.
- You cause harmless tremors in the ground for 1 minute, as per the thumaturgy spell.
- At your touch, you mold a portion of loose earth or loose stone into shapes as you designate for up to 1 hour.
- You attempt to affect magical earth or stone using one of mold earth's other abilities. When you attempt to manipulate magical earth or stone this way, you must make a spellcasting ability check against a DC equal to the spell's level plus the caster's spell save DC; or against a DC of the DM's choice. On a failed check, nothing happens.

At the DM's option, you may attempt to manipulate earth or stone in a manner not described by this feat, but it should be no more powerful than the effects described above, and the effects must be within range and fit within a 5-foot cube. Your DM may ask for a relevant ability check or saving throw in order for the effect to work. The DM has final say on such a use of your abilities.

If you cast mold earth multiple times, you can have any number of non-instantaneous effects created by it active at a time, and you can dismiss such an effect as a bonus action. All such effects cease should you fall unconscious.

**Sharpshooter**

*Prerequisites: Proficiency with weapons with the range property*

Your training has made you more deadly with ranged weapons. You gain the following benefits:

- Increase your Dexterity score by 1 to a maximum of 20.
- You can reroll 1s on damage rolls made with ranged weapons. You must use the new roll, even if it is a 1.
- You score a critical hit on 19-20 when wielding a ranged weapon.

*Prerequisites: Dexterity or Strength of 13 or higher*


While wielding a ranged weapon with which you are proficient, as a reaction, you may make an attack against a creature that moves 5 feet while it is within 30 feet of you.

**Spry**

*Prerequisites: Dexterity 13*

You have trained to be quick on your feet and hard to hit. You gain the following benefits:
- Your speed increases by 5 feet.
- While you are wearing no armor and not wielding a shield, your AC equals 10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Proficiency Bonus.
- When you move on your turn in combat, you can double your speed until the end of the turn. Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you move 0 feet on one of your turns.

**Technician**

*Prerequisites: Must be able to cast at least one 1st, 2nd, or 3rd level spell*

You have superior arcane knowledge of one of your lower level spells, and you aren't afraid to flaunt it.
- Choose one spell that is not a cantrip, is 3rd level or below, and lets you roll a die (e.g. magic missile). The die for that spell is increased by 1 size.
- The Dice must be used for a spell's damage or healing.

For example, for Ray of Sickness, which is normally 2d8, it would go to 2d10. For Magic Missile, which is normally 3 bolts of 1d4+1, it'd be 3 bolts of 1d6+1. 

**Third Hand Marksman**

*Prerequisites: Access to the mage hand spell, Dexterity 13 or higher.*

You can use your magic hand in synchrony with your shooting skills.
- When your mage hand is active, you gain one additional interaction with object on your turn to interact with a ranged weapon.
- You ignore the loading property of weapons, and you can use your reaction to reload a weapon with the reload property when your mage hand is active.
- You can use your mage hand and one of your hands to hold a ranged weapon with the two handed property, keeping one of your hands free.

**Special**. If you use the Matthew Mercer rules for firearms, you also gain the following benefit:
- While your mage hand is active, you can use your bonus action to reload or repair a misfired weapon.

**Wand Master**

Through extensive research and long hours of practice you have mastered the use of wands, gaining the following benefits:
- You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and to saving throw DCs with spells cast from magic wands.
- Magic wands you are attuned to have 1 extra charge.
- You can hold a wand in each hand. When you use an action to expend a charge with one wand, you can use your bonus action to expend a charge with the other wand. Doing so expends an additional charge from both wands.

**Quick Learner**

You are extremely apt to learn new skills. You gain the following benefits:
- Increase an ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You have advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks when trying to learn new information or performing research on topics.
- When learning a new tool, feat, spell, or proficiency such as a skill, weapon, or language, reduce the time it would take to learn them by half.

**Utility Magic Caster**

*Prerequisites: Charisma, Wisdom, or Intelligence 13 or higher*

You can give practical everyday uses to your magical abilities. You gain the following benefits:
- When you choose this feat, you choose a 1st-level spell of your choice that has the ritual tag. You can cast this spell only as a ritual, and you may change your spell selection out with another spell when you finish a long rest. Your choice of Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell.
- You learn two cantrips that can not deal damage from any spell list of your choice. Your choice of Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells.
